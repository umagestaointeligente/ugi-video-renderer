# Orbit Media Labs — Production Policy v1

## Operating model
- CEO: human strategic direction, financial approvals, identity verification and account-recovery actions.
- Operator: Lola Magic Engine executes research, scoring, planning, production, QA, publishing, analytics and learning autonomously wherever platform permissions allow.
- Cost rule: zero-cost-first; no paid fallback without explicit CEO approval.

## Active channels now
### Você Sabia Agora?
- Target: 3 short-form posts/day.
- Platforms: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, TikTok once credentials are connected.
- Content: current curiosities, science, history, technology, nature, space, behavior and verified facts.
- Sources/visuals: original, public-domain or separately verified commercial-use assets.
- Monetization-safe principle: each video must contain original narrative, structure, fact-checking, editing and value; no mass-produced generic slideshow pattern.

### Cena Certa
- Target: 2 short-form posts/day.
- Platforms: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, TikTok once credentials are connected.
- Content: film/series commentary, scene analysis, behind-the-scenes facts and contextualized storytelling.
- Rights gate: autonomous publication is blocked for raw film/TV clips unless a documented lawful rights basis exists. Default production uses commentary/analysis with original narration and independently licensed/public-domain visuals where necessary.
- No simple scene reposts intended to imitate ownership.

## Future channels after account release
- Fala Que Vale: 2 posts/day; permission/license or materially transformative review required for third-party interview footage.
- Joga Tudo: 2 posts/day; no broadcast-footage reupload without rights basis.
- MODO ZEN: 1 long-form original video/day initially, with later optimization by retention/watch time.

## Thumbnail integrity gate
THUMBNAIL_SEMANTIC_MATCH_REQUIRED=true
CLICKBAIT_MISMATCH=HARD_FAIL

Every thumbnail must:
1. depict the actual topic, event, object, person/character category or emotion delivered by the video;
2. avoid implying footage/facts that are absent from the content;
3. use strong visual contrast while preserving truthfulness;
4. be generated after topic research, not from a fixed generic template;
5. be blocked if text/image promise and video content materially diverge.

## Content gates before publishing
1. TREND_SCORE
2. VIRALITY_SCORE
3. RIGHTS_GATE
4. FACT_GATE
5. THUMBNAIL_SEMANTIC_MATCH
6. QA_GATE
7. COST_GATE
8. PLATFORM_AUTH

Any hard gate failure blocks publishing.

## Initial 30-day cadence
- Você Sabia Agora?: 3/day = 90 posts/month.
- Cena Certa: 2/day = 60 posts/month.
- Total current production target: 150 short-form masters/month, then adapted per platform.
- Each master receives platform-specific metadata, aspect-ratio validation and posting-time optimization; we do not create four unrelated videos when one adapted master is sufficient.

## Learning loop
For every post capture: impressions/reach, views, 3-second hold where available, average view duration, completion rate, likes, comments, shares, saves, subscribers/follows gained, CTR where applicable and monetization metrics when enabled.

Lola Magic Engine compares creative hypothesis, topic, hook, thumbnail, length and posting window, then increases/decreases future allocation. Winning patterns become controlled templates; semantic topic and creative execution must still vary to avoid inauthentic mass-produced content.
