# LSI Capital Intelligence — Source Hierarchy R1

## Purpose
Build a source-first investment research layer for Brazil plus global capital-allocation principles. This file is research governance, not investment advice and not authority to execute real orders.

## Tier 1 — Primary / official Brazil
1. CVM Open Data: regulated entities, public-company filings, fund documents, offerings and enforcement datasets.
2. B3: historical quotations, index methodologies, trading/market structure and listed-instrument reference data.
3. Banco Central do Brasil: Copom/Selic, Focus expectations, SGS/time series, inflation/credit/external-sector monetary data.
4. Tesouro Direto / Tesouro Nacional: official public-bond prices, rates, historical series and rules.
5. IBGE/SIDRA: IPCA/INPC, GDP, labor, industry, retail and demographic statistics.

## Tier 2 — Professional Brazilian market references
6. ANBIMA/ANBIMA Data: IMA, IDkA, IDA, fund and capital-market reference data and methodologies.
7. FGV/IBRE: IGP and economic confidence/activity indicators.

## Tier 3 — Company truth
8. CVM issuer filings (DFP, ITR, FRE, material facts).
9. Company investor-relations releases only after reconciliation with filed numbers when material.

## Tier 4 — Aggregation / research
10. Bigdata.com for structured company/ETF discovery, filings, earnings, broker research and news; never overrides primary filings.
11. Reputable financial press as context, not source of record for financial statements.

## Buffett / Berkshire primary curriculum
- Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letters 1977–2024.
- Berkshire Hathaway Owner's Manual.
- Berkshire annual/interim reports and SEC filings.
- Warren Buffett / Charlie Munger special letters and meeting materials when officially hosted.

## Derived Buffett/Munger rules for LSI
- Think as an owner, not a ticker renter.
- Intrinsic value and future cash generation matter more than short-term quotation.
- Require understandable economics and durable competitive advantage.
- Management integrity and capital allocation are first-class variables.
- Demand a margin of safety; uncertainty must reduce position size or block entry.
- Opportunity cost is explicit: every risky asset competes with cash/Treasuries and the best available alternative.
- Concentration is permitted only after evidence rises; never confuse conviction with certainty.
- Avoid leverage that can force liquidation.
- Prefer long holding periods when the business thesis remains intact.
- Record mistakes and thesis breaks explicitly.
